What we do

Everything you expect from a pharmacy — with none of the runaround.

Bring us a new prescription, move an existing one over, or just stop in with a question. If we don’t have something in stock, we’ll usually have it for you the next business day.

New & refill prescriptions

Drop off, e-scripts from your doctor, and refills. Most prescriptions are filled while you wait.

Free transfers

Switching from a chain pharmacy? Give us your name and current pharmacy — we handle the paperwork and the phone call. No charge.

Vaccinations

Flu, COVID, shingles, and routine immunizations. Call ahead to check availability and whether you need an appointment.

Talk to a pharmacist

Free, no appointment needed. Questions about side effects, interactions, or what to take for a cold — just ask.

Over-the-counter & everyday needs

Delivery & curbside

Common questions

Good to know

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans, including Medicaid and Medicare Part D. If you’re not sure, call us with your card handy and we’ll check in a minute.

How long does a prescription take?

Most are ready in about 15 minutes. If we need to call your doctor or order the medication, we’ll let you know right away and give you a realistic time.

Can I transfer prescriptions from CVS, Walgreens, or Rite Aid?

Yes — and it’s free. Just call us with your name, date of birth, and current pharmacy. We take care of the rest.

What if you don’t have my medication in stock?

We order daily. In most cases we can have it for you the next business day, and we’ll call you as soon as it arrives.
